## Rules and migration decisions

- Native RVN uses witness-v2 ML-DSA-44 spending under bit 12. Historical
  classical RVN outputs remain spendable under their original rule. Owners
  migrate voluntarily by moving them to new PQ outputs.
- Mainnet bit 12 starts no earlier than 1 July 2027 by median time past,
  times out on 1 January 2029, and requires 1714 signaling blocks in a
  2016-block window. After lock-in and activation, the block-weight limit
  rises from 8 to 12 MWU with a contextual PQ witness discount. It rises
  to 16 MWU at activation height plus 525600 blocks. An active PQ sigop
  costs two units. Core 4.8.0 may reject blocks accepted by the upgraded
  rule, so validators and dependent services must upgrade before activation.
- Upgraded mainnet mining templates signal bit 12 automatically during
  STARTED and LOCKED_IN. Signaling does not upgrade other validators.
  Operator readiness and the go/no-go decision are due before the original
  STARTED window.
- Bit 13 applies to assets and owner tokens only after bit 12 and the bit-8
  transfer-script rule are ACTIVE. Its mainnet window starts no earlier than
  1 January 2029, times out on 1 July 2030, and requires 1714 of 2016
  signaling blocks. A new protected asset output is spent with a matching
  native RVN witness-v2 input signed by ML-DSA-44; the asset input itself
  needs no ECDSA signature. Historical asset and owner-token outputs retain
  their classical spending rule until owners migrate voluntarily.
- No activation retroactively protects historical holdings. There is no
  automatic network-wide point when every remaining classical output
  becomes unspendable.

Public testnet force-enables native PQ from genesis. Its separate bit-13
window is 1 April 2027 to 1 October 2028 with a 1310-of-2016 threshold.
That testnet override cannot demonstrate the mainnet 8-to-12-MWU BIP9
transition; controlled boundary regressions cover that transition.

## Known limits and remaining work

1. FINDING-067 remains HIGH and OPEN for the bit-12 operator go/no-go,
   readiness of miners, pools, full nodes, exchanges, and other services,
   and final stable-release steps. If readiness is absent, revise both
   deployment dates in a replacement release, qualify that release, and
   distribute it before the original bit-12 STARTED window.
   FINDING-197 is fixed on the tested `a756574d4` PR head. Before the
   historical height gate, a declared KAWPOW height could demand a very
   large verifier context.
   The local `-maxkawpowcontextmb` budget (default 256 MiB per context)
   reports an error without invalidating the header or penalizing the
   peer. Commits `a147a994d` and `ccb576e13` require each KAWPOW claim to meet its
   target before a context is built and remember completed proof
   results, so a replayed header cannot force new context work. Peer
   input can hold at most one context within the budget. A
   checkpoint-anchored scan of the canonical mainnet block files found
   that all 3,268,040 KAWPOW headers up to the gate declare their actual
   height, so the largest canonical context needs 91 MiB. Build Raven
   and Final Gate passed on `a756574d4`; its five bundles still need
   independent review.
   FINDING-199 blocked the Windows Final Gate `40455702781` on
   `30349977b`: the standalone consensus library inherited a node
   argument reference from the KAWPOW budget check. Commit `c4d1b20ad`
   removes that dependency while preserving the node option. The
   `aa5b128c5` exact-SHA Windows Final Gate passed, so this finding is
   fixed. Commit `16c2d00a1` moves the option to startup and makes ELF builds
   reject unresolved symbols in the shared library. That check exposed
   an unresolved `sha256_transform` in 32-bit ARM builds; commit
   `c86a18d96` compiles the existing C transform there, and every ELF
   Final Gate variant on `a756574d4` linked under the check. Any later
   source or documentation change needs its own final-SHA CI pair and
   package review under FINDING-067.
2. Official PR #1330 needs maintainer review before merge. The historical
   PR #1281 remains a separate proposal with a different branch history.
3. Native Windows and macOS GUI operation has not been exercised. Community
   testing after publication is assigned for first launch, wallet recovery,
   asset and owner-token operations, and shutdown. This is a disclosed
   limitation, not a claim of native GUI verification.
4. Some protected asset spends consume the matching native PQ anchor
   without refreshing it. The owner can fund the matching PQ address again;
   consensus rejects a later spend without an anchor. `fundrawtransaction`
   does not select matching anchors automatically. Raw callers must provide
   them and inspect the final transaction. See the asset design note and
   release notes for the exact cases.
5. Before a stable release, freeze its actual final SHA, rerun the release
   contract and `make check`, obtain green Build Raven and Final Gate runs
   on that SHA, and independently review all five packages. Follow the
   release process for separate owner decisions on the stable tag and
   publication, and for final signatures and signed checksums. Do not use
   the RC1 or RC2 qualification as evidence for a later branch head.

The detailed private audit and local raw evidence are intentionally kept
outside the repository. This public handoff contains no wallet material,
credentials, private file paths, or private test data.

## Change discipline

New commits and local merge commits on the owner's branches must record
ALENOC as both author and committer. Verify those identities in the commit
range before pushing. Do not rewrite published history or force-push
without explicit owner authorization.
